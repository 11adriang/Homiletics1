How the manuals work

Every manual is interactive: select any phrase to highlight it in four colors, bold, underline, or attach a note, and each lesson ends with a space for your own class notes. Use Export and Import inside a manual to carry your marks from one device to another. Everything saves in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
